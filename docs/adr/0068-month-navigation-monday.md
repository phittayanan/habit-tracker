# 0068. Month arrows, and weeks start on Monday

Status: chosen by phittayanan on 2026-11-27 in [Issue #151](https://github.com/phittayanan/habit-tracker/issues/151) (Q5, Q12). To be agreed by projectnewy in the review of the #151 PR.

## Decision

1. Arrows ‹ and › beside the month title move to earlier months and forward up to the current month (Asia/Bangkok), not past it. The List (F-15) shows the month the calendar shows.
2. The calendar week starts on Monday.

## Options considered

- Months: **A. ‹ › arrows (chosen)**; B. the current month only (rejected: on 1 December November could not be seen).
- Week start: **Monday (chosen)**; Sunday, as in the prototype.
