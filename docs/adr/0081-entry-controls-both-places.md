# 0081. Edit and Delete are in the day box and on the goals page

Status: chosen by projectnewy on 2026-11-27 in [Issue #150](https://github.com/phittayanan/habit-tracker/issues/150) (Q7). Agreed by phittayanan in the [review of 507ba1c](https://github.com/phittayanan/habit-tracker/pull/171) of PR #171. Answers F-11 OP-11.10. Amends ADR 0073 point 2: the goals page also gains these controls.

## Decision

Each entry has Edit and Delete on the goals page and in the calendar's day box or panel (F-11).

## Options considered

- **A. Both places (chosen).**
- B. The goals page only.
- C. The day box only.

## Why

A Participant can fix an entry where they see it.
