# 0088. The reminder is set and shown on the goals page

Status: chosen by projectnewy on 2026-11-27 in [Issue #157](https://github.com/phittayanan/habit-tracker/issues/157) (Q3). To be agreed by phittayanan in the review of the #157 PR.

## Decision

Each goal on the goals page shows its reminder and has the controls to set, change or remove it in place. This amends ADR 0073 point 2: the goals page also gains these controls.

## Options considered

- A. The goals page (chosen).
- B. A goal detail page.

## Why

It is quicker to build and does not wait for the detail page of F-16.
